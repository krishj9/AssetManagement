# Governance and Responsible AI Rules

## Mandatory controls
- Human approval is required before any trade proposal becomes actionable
- No hidden rationale may be presented as fact
- No unsupported or fabricated market claims
- No bypass of mandate, suitability, or compliance rules

## Audit
- Log every approval, rejection, override, and recommendation issuance
- Log memory reads and writes with category and confidence metadata
- Log all compliance verdicts and exceptions

## Explainability
- Show structured rationale summaries, not chain-of-thought
- Every recommendation must include evidence and rule checks
